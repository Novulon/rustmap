How to find your Steam ID64:

  1. Open Steam, click your profile name at the top right, and select View Profile.
  2. Copy the link (URL) from your browser address bar.
  3. If the link ends in a 17-digit number starting with 765611..., that is your Steam ID64.
  4. If you have a custom profile link, paste it into steamid.io to get your numeric ID64.

How to get your Rust+ Player Token:

  1. Open Command Prompt and register your credentials (one-time setup):
    npx @liamcottle/rustplus.js fcm-register
    Complete the Steam login in the browser window that opens.
  2. In Rust, press Esc, select SESSION, and click PAIRING. If a popup asks to allow push notifications, click the green ENABLE button.
  3. Ignore mobile app links: You do not need to download or open the iOS/Android phone app.
  4. Start the listener in Command Prompt:
    npx @liamcottle/rustplus.js fcm-listen
  5. Click PAIRING in Rust. Look inside the body section of the terminal output for "playerToken":"123456789" and copy just the number sequence inside the quotes.

How BattleMetrics Search works:

  1. Type all or part of your server's name (e.g., Rustafied, Reddit Main) into the box.
  2. Click Search to query BattleMetrics for active matching Rust servers.
  3. Click any server from the dropdown results to select it and update your active target server in Firebase.

How Self Highlighting works:

  1. Enter your exact Steam name or 64-bit Steam ID in the input box.
  2. When active player data loads on the map, your marker will be highlighted in orange with an animated radar pulse.
  3. Your preference is automatically saved locally in your browser so you don't have to re-enter it next time.
